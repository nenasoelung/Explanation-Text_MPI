Material: What is Explanation Text?

Materi Pembelajaran Lengkap Explanation Text (Social Function, Generic Structure, & Language Features)

Definition (Pengertian)

Explanation Text is a non-fiction text that tells how or why natural, scientific, or social phenomena happen.

(Explanation Text adalah teks ilmiah non-fiksi yang menjelaskan proses 'bagaimana' atau 'mengapa' suatu fenomena alam atau ilmiah dapat terjadi).

Social Function (Tujuan Sosial)

To explain the processes involved in the formation or working of natural, social, or scientific phenomena.

(Tujuannya adalah untuk menjelaskan urutan proses terjadinya suatu peristiwa alam seperti gunung meletus, hujan, pelangi, tsunami, dll).

Generic Structure (Struktur Teks)

Part 1

General Statement

Pernyataan umum yang memperkenalkan fenomena alam yang akan dijelaskan (misalnya: definisi gunung berapi).

Part 2

Sequenced Explanation

Serangkaian paragraf yang menjelaskan langkah-langkah atau sebab-akibat terjadinya fenomena secara berurutan.

Part 3

Closing / Conclusion

Kesimpulan singkat atau penutup dari keseluruhan penjelasan fenomena (bersifat opsional).

Language Features (Ciri Kebahasaan)

1. Simple Present Tense

Menggunakan fakta umum. (e.g., Magma collects in a chamber.)

2. Passive Voice

Fokus pada objek peristiwa. (e.g., Ash is thrown high into the sky.)

3. Sequential Connectives

Kata hubung waktu: First, then, next, finally, as a result.

4. Action Verbs

Kata kerja tindakan: Erupts, flows, melts, forms, rises.

Interactive Text Analyzer

A volcanic eruption is a powerful natural phenomenon where magma, ash, and gases escape from beneath the Earth's crust onto the surface.

Deep underground, extreme heat melts rock to form liquid magma. First, magma collects in a chamber beneath the volcano. As pressure builds up, the magma is forced upwards through cracks and vents in the rocks.

When the pressure becomes too high, the volcano erupts. Hot lava, poisonous gas, and volcanic ash are blasted into the atmosphere. As a result, lava flows down the mountain slopes, cooling into solid rock over time.

Reading Passage: How Volcanic Eruptions Happen

A volcanic eruption occurs when pressure beneath Earth's crust forces molten rock, known as magma, to rise to the surface. Deep inside the Earth, temperatures are hot enough to melt rocks into a thick liquid. Because magma is lighter than the solid rock around it, it rises and collects in magma chambers beneath volcanoes.

Over time, gas dissolved in the magma expands, creating intense pressure. When this pressure becomes too strong, the magma escapes through vents and craters. As a result, ash, hot gas, and liquid lava are forcefully ejected into the air. Eventually, the lava cools down on the slopes, building up layers that form the shape of a volcanic mountain.
